IRONSIGHT data sources

All data comes from free, public endpoints and needs no API keys. IRONSIGHT does not own any of it: every feed is credited to its provider.

News and Telegram

News comes from per-theater RSS feeds: major wires and broadcasters, regional outlets and defense publications. Telegram coverage is public channels only, scraped through Telegram's public embed endpoint. The full channel lists are in the README. See the theater pages for Iran / Israel and Russia / Ukraine.
