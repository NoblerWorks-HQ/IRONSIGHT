Iran / Israel theater

The Iran / Israel theater is IRONSIGHT's default view, covering the Middle East.

  • Air-raid alerts. Israeli Home Front Command (Pikud HaOref) sirens through the Tzeva Adom community mirror, with audio notifications and on-map sirens and arcs. Polled every 15 seconds.
  • Theater map. Military aircraft from adsb.lol, naval vessels across the Persian Gulf, Red Sea and Eastern Mediterranean, strike markers from news and Telegram, missile trajectory arcs, range rings, country borders and a distance-measurement tool.
  • Telegram OSINT. Public channels from Israeli, Iranian, Gulf and independent sources, auto-translated from Hebrew, Arabic and Farsi.
  • Conflict monitor. Categorized events (strikes, defense, diplomatic, nuclear) with per-theater geocoding, weapon-type classification and severity scoring.
  • Regional threat monitor. Per-country threat levels across neighboring states.
  • Markets. Defense stocks, indices, VIX, gold, USD, oil and gas, crypto, and Polymarket odds relevant to the conflict.
  • Satellite thermal. NASA FIRMS fire and explosion detection filtered to the region.
