Russia / Ukraine theater

The Russia / Ukraine theater re-points every panel, map layer and feed to Eastern Europe.

  • Drone and missile tracker. Real-time Shahed drone, cruise and ballistic missile, and glide-bomb (KAB) tracks from Neptun, with heading, movement trails and confidence, drawn on the map and listed in the alert panel. Polled every 20 seconds.
  • Air-raid alerts. Ukrainian oblast alert status from alerts.com.ua with audio notifications. Polled every 15 seconds.
  • Theater map. Military aircraft, naval vessels in the Black Sea and Sea of Azov, strike markers, range rings, country borders and a distance tool.
  • Telegram OSINT. Ukrainian official and military channels, Ukrainian and Russian outlets, independent journalists, Russian state and war correspondents, and OSINT aggregators, auto-translated from Ukrainian and Russian.
  • News. Per-theater RSS from international wires and Ukrainian, Russian and independent outlets.
  • Markets and satellite. Defense and energy markets, Polymarket odds, and NASA FIRMS thermal detection for the region.
