# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Critique code and ideas, not people. Assume good faith, and remember that most contributors here are working on this in their spare time.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient with questions that seem basic.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This parser drops the last order" is useful. "Whoever wrote this does not understand parsing" is not.
- Cite sources or show a reproduction when you report a problem.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Using issues or pull requests primarily to advertise a product or service.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer, **[@PatrickWiloak](https://github.com/PatrickWiloak)**, through the organization's [private reporting process](https://github.com/NoblerWorks-HQ/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md) or by direct message on any of the channels linked from the [README](./README.md). Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit, delete, or decline any contribution, and may block an account, for behavior that breaches this document.
